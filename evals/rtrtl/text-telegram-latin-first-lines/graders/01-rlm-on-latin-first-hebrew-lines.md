The fixed summary.py puts a U+200F RIGHT-TO-LEFT MARK at the start of a deal line when that line begins with a Latin product name (such as "Galaxy S24" or "AirPods Pro"), and the fix uses RLM rather than FSI/PDI isolates (U+2066–U+2069), embeddings or overrides (U+202A–U+202E).
