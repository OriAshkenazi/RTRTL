# Contributing

Contributions are welcome for new RTL language coverage, stacks, fonts, renderers, and corrections. Compatibility tests for a target language are especially useful; Hebrew results do not certify another language.

## Evidence to include

- The target language, writing system/script, and orthographic variant under test.
- A small, synthetic sample in that language that exercises mixed LTR tokens, numbers, punctuation, and relevant script features. Include exact reproduction steps; do not use private project material.
- The actual font and version, application/library and version, operating system, and export path/version.
- Separate results for source/structure, visual appearance, interaction, and search/copy. State what remains untested and any limits or failures.

Do not submit confidential documents, identifying project details, or excerpts from private issue history. Replace real content with a minimal synthetic example that still reproduces the behavior. Avoid broad claims from a single renderer or version; state the tested scope.

For a new stack, describe its native direction controls, mixed-script handling, font behavior, export path, and known limitations. Keep claims scoped to the tested language, script, variant, and versions. For corrections, point to the affected guidance and include evidence that changes the conclusion.
