# Contributing

Contributions are welcome for new RTL stacks, fonts, renderers, and corrections to existing guidance. Keep changes focused and evidence-based.

## Evidence to include

- A small, synthetic, reproducible case with no private project data or history.
- The relevant application, library, operating-system, and font versions, plus the steps needed to reproduce the result.
- Visual evidence and source/structure evidence as separate findings. For search and copy behavior, report those results separately too.
- What you checked, what you did not check, and any limits or known failures.

Do not submit confidential documents, identifying project details, or excerpts from private issue history. Replace real content with a minimal synthetic example that still reproduces the behavior. Avoid broad claims from a single renderer or version; state the tested scope.

For a new stack, describe its native direction controls, mixed-script handling, font behavior, export path, and known limitations. For corrections, point to the affected guidance and include evidence that changes the conclusion.
