Design inspiration

The site combines poster-scale typography with soft, secondary gradient fields, drawing inspiration from Awwwards’ gradients in web design and free fonts collection. Layout, wordmark and implementation are original.

Typefaces

Geist by Vercel and Noto Sans Hebrew are self-hosted here. Both are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Original licenses: Geist OFL and Noto Sans Hebrew OFL. Font files come from the Google Fonts repository.
