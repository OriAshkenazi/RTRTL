Design inspiration

The visual design pairs expressive typography with subtle gradients. It draws inspiration from Awwwards’ articles on gradients in web design and a collection of free fonts. The layout, logo and implementation are original.

Typefaces

This site hosts Geist by Vercel and Noto Sans Hebrew. The Arabic edition uses Noto Sans Arabic. All three are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Read the original licenses: Geist OFL, Noto Sans Hebrew OFL and Noto Sans Arabic OFL. The font files come from the Google Fonts repository.
